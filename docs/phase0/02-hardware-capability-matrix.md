source/headers/commands; UNVERIFIED `[U]` = plausible but not checked, or not

## Do not claim

- ❌ That `CONFIG_SPIRAM` is enabled, or any specific PSRAM size/type/speed
  for any board (default is off; capacity is runtime-detected `[U]`; 250 MHz
  PSRAM requires silicon rev ≥ v3).
- ❌ Any measured performance number (PPA/DMA2D throughput, PSRAM bandwidth,
  tile rate, concurrency scaling) — nothing was benchmarked; no P4
  performance figure appears anywhere in Phase 0 docs.
- ❌ RVV / vector extensions, `zpn`, `zdsp`, or compiler auto-vectorization
  (rejected by the toolchain; PIE SIMD is asm-only).
- ❌ `esp_async_memcpy_new`, `esp_async_memcpy_install_gdma_ext_mem`,
  `driver/gdma.h` (as public), or `esp_dma_utils`-era helpers in IDF 6.1.1.
- ❌ That the ILI9341/ILI9488 drivers work on P4 (Arduino/Teensy-only).
- ❌ That internal SRAM is DMA-coherent (it is L1-cached).
- ❌ That DPI accepts ARGB8888 input or more than 3 framebuffers.
- ❌ Hardware validation of any kind — nothing was flashed or run on a chip.
- ❌ That a specific panel/touch controller is supported hardware (the
  managed components in `example/` are scaffold dependencies).
